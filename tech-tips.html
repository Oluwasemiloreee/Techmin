PRODUCTIVITY

Use keyboard shortcuts to move faster

Master copy, paste, search, tab switching, screenshots, and window management on your computer.

Start with Ctrl/Cmd + K for search, Alt/Command + Tab for switching apps, and Windows + Shift + S for screenshots.
SECURITY

Turn on two-factor authentication

Add a second layer of protection to your email, social, banking, and work accounts.

Use an authenticator app when available. Save backup codes somewhere secure and offline.
AI

Give AI better instructions

Get stronger results by adding context, the desired outcome, constraints, and an example.

Use: “Act as…”, “Help me achieve…”, “Keep it within…”, and “Use this example…”
MOBILE

Scan documents with your phone

Create clean PDF scans without buying a scanner or installing complicated software.

Use Notes on iPhone or Google Drive on Android to scan, crop, enhance, and save as PDF.
BACKUP

Follow the 3-2-1 backup rule

Keep important work safe when a device is lost, damaged, stolen, or infected.

Keep 3 copies, on 2 different storage types, with 1 copy stored away from your main device.
INTERNET

Improve a slow Wi-Fi connection

Simple steps can fix dead zones, buffering, and unstable calls before you replace your router.

Move the router higher and centrally, restart it, reduce interference, and test both 2.4GHz and 5GHz bands.
